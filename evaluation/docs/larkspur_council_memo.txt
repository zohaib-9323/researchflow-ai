Larkspur Town Council - Finance Committee Memo on Solar Expansion (April 2025)

Purpose
This memo reviews the proposal to expand the Community Solar Pilot to 1,000 homes.

Assessment of savings
The finance committee re-analysed the pilot meter data after excluding eleven homes that also installed heat pumps during the pilot period. With those homes excluded, the average reduction in grid electricity consumption was 12 percent rather than the 18 percent stated in the pilot report.

Budget position
The committee estimates that expanding to 1,000 homes would cost approximately 4.8 million dollars without battery storage. Adding batteries to 200 homes would add a further 1.5 million dollars.

Position
The committee supports a smaller expansion to 500 homes in 2026, with a decision on further expansion after a second year of data. The committee agrees with the pilot team that roof condition surveys should be completed before installation.
