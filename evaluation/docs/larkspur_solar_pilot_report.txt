Larkspur Community Solar Pilot - Final Report (2025)

Summary
The Larkspur Community Solar Pilot ran from March 2024 to February 2025. The pilot installed 240 rooftop solar systems on residential homes in the Eastgate and Millbrook neighbourhoods. The total installed capacity was 1.9 megawatts.

Results
Participating households reduced their average grid electricity consumption by 18 percent over the twelve-month pilot period. The largest reductions occurred between May and August, when participating homes used 31 percent less grid electricity than the comparison group. Winter months showed a reduction of only 6 percent.

Costs and funding
The pilot cost 1.2 million dollars in total. Sixty percent of the funding came from the Larkspur Green Futures Fund and the remaining forty percent came from a regional energy transition grant. The average installation cost per home was 4,100 dollars after bulk purchasing discounts.

Challenges
Installers reported that 22 homes required roof repairs before panels could be installed, which delayed those installations by an average of seven weeks. Battery storage was not included in the pilot because of budget limits.

Recommendations
The pilot team recommends expanding the programme to 1,000 homes by 2027 and adding battery storage to at least 200 of them.
