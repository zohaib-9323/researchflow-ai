Larkspur Groundwater Quality Survey (2025)

The survey sampled 64 private wells across Larkspur between June and September 2025.

Nitrate levels
Nine of the 64 wells (14 percent) exceeded the regulatory nitrate limit of 10 milligrams per litre. All nine wells were located within two kilometres of the Hollow Creek agricultural district.

Recommendations
The survey recommends annual testing of wells in the Hollow Creek area and a public information campaign on well-head protection. Residents with affected wells were offered free filtration units.
